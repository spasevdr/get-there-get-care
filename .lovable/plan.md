# Detroit Ride Navigator - Plan with gaps filled

## What your plan already covers well
Clear single problem, 5 questions, no login or personal data, honest "Potential option" wording, source links, test scenarios, and a short pitch.

## What is missing (add these)

**1. The real program data (biggest gap)**
The prompt has a placeholder. Before step 2, collect 3-5 verified programs, for example: Medicaid health plan transportation benefit (Michigan NEMT through each plan), Medicare Advantage ride benefits, DDOT MetroLift (paratransit, wheelchair), SMART Connector, and local nonprofit or hospital ride programs. Record for each: who it serves, service area, wheelchair access, booking notice, cost, phone, official link, date checked.

**2. Urgent and emergency paths**
- "Is this a medical emergency?" note: call 911.
- Appointment today: most insurance rides need 2-3 days notice, so show same-day backups (bus, rideshare, clinic social worker).

**3. Missing questions or answers**
- Medicaid health plan name (rides are booked through the plan) with "I don't know" plus where to find it on the card.
- "Unsure" for every question, not only some.
- Age 60+ (senior ride programs) and whether a caregiver rides along.

**4. Help with "what to have ready"**
Checklist items: insurance card, appointment date, time and clinic address, doctor's name, pickup address, mobility needs, and a script of what to say on the call.

**5. Language and access**
Plain language (6th-grade reading level), Spanish and Arabic toggle (common in Detroit), tap-to-call phone links, works on slow phones.

**6. Safety and trust text**
Short disclaimer: not medical or legal advice, info may change, confirm with the program. Data-checked date on the page.

**7. Clinic staff angle**
A one-line note that navigators or front-desk staff can run the tool with a patient. Strengthens the pitch.

**8. Presentation numbers**
One local fact (missed appointments due to transportation) and how you would measure success in a pilot.

## Suggested build order
1. Fictional version (your first prompt).
2. Add urgent path, unsure answers, checklist script.
3. Swap in verified programs.
4. Run the three scenarios plus a "today, uninsured" one.
5. Publish and get a shareable link for the demo.
